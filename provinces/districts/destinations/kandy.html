Discover Kandy

The cultural heart of Sri Lanka.

Kandy was the last capital of the ancient Kandyan Kingdom and remains one of Sri Lanka's most important cultural destinations.

The city is centred around the historic Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy Lake and the former Royal Palace complex.

Beyond the city, Kandy District stretches into mountains, forests, tea country, waterfalls, historic temples and traditional villages.

CeylonTrails brings these destinations together in one place so travellers can discover Kandy one destination at a time.

Travel Questions

Kandy FAQ

What is Kandy famous for?

Kandy is famous for its cultural heritage, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy Lake, Kandyan traditions and surrounding mountains.

How many places can I visit in Kandy?

Kandy District contains a wide range of heritage, religious, natural, mountain and scenic attractions. CeylonTrails will continue expanding this directory as more verified destinations are added.

Is Kandy only a city?

No. Kandy District includes many destinations outside the city centre, including villages, mountains, forests, waterfalls and tea areas.

Can I travel around Kandy without a car?

Yes. Trains, buses, tuk-tuks and taxis can be useful, although remote mountain destinations may require additional transport.
