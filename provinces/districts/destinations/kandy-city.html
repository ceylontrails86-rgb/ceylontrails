Kandy City

Discover Sri Lanka's cultural capital — where ancient heritage, beautiful mountains, temples, nature and local life come together.

Discover Kandy

The cultural heart of Sri Lanka.

Kandy is one of Sri Lanka's most iconic destinations, surrounded by green mountains and centred around the beautiful Kandy Lake.

The city combines history, culture, religion, nature and local life, making it an ideal place to begin exploring the Central Province.

From historic temples and gardens to mountain trails and scenic viewpoints, Kandy offers something for every type of traveller.
