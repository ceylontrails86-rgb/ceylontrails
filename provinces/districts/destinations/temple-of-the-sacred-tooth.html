Temple of the Sacred Tooth

Discover one of Sri Lanka's most important cultural and religious landmarks in the heart of historic Kandy.

Kandy Heritage

A place of history, culture and devotion.

The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, commonly known as Sri Dalada Maligawa, is located in the historic city of Kandy.

The temple is an important part of Sri Lanka's cultural and religious heritage and attracts visitors from around the world.

Its architecture, traditions and surrounding Kandy Lake landscape make it one of the city's most memorable places to experience.
