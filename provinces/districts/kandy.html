Discover Kandy

The cultural heart of Sri Lanka.

Kandy is one of Sri Lanka's most important cultural and historic destinations. The city was the last royal capital of the island.

The historic centre of Kandy is closely connected with the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy Lake and the former Royal Palace complex.

Beyond the city centre, Kandy District opens into green mountains, forests, gardens, villages and historic temples.

CeylonTrails brings these places together so travellers can explore Kandy place by place.

Getting Around

Tuk-tuks, buses, trains, taxis and private vehicles are useful for exploring Kandy and its surrounding areas.

🚂

Train Travel

Kandy is an important railway hub and can be reached by train from Colombo and other parts of Sri Lanka.

🌤️

Weather

Kandy has a cooler hill-country climate than much of Sri Lanka. Weather can change quickly in the mountains.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kandy famous for?

Kandy is famous for its cultural heritage, the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy Lake, Kandyan traditions and the surrounding mountain landscape.

What can I see in Kandy?

Visitors can explore religious and historic sites, botanical gardens, forests, mountains, scenic viewpoints and local cultural experiences.

Is Kandy only a city destination?

No. Kandy District includes many areas outside the city centre, including mountain, nature, heritage and village destinations.

Can I explore Kandy without a car?

Yes. Public transport, trains, buses, tuk-tuks and taxis can be useful, although some remote mountain destinations may require additional transport.

Ready to explore Kandy?

Start with the Temple of the Sacred Tooth, then continue through Kandy's mountains, gardens, temples, villages and hidden trails.
